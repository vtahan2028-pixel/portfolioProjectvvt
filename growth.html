My Growth

At the beginning of this course, I did not know very much about coding or building websites. As the class continued, I learned how HTML creates the structure of a webpage and how CSS changes its appearance.

One area in which I grew was problem-solving. Sometimes my links, images, sprites, or other parts of a project did not work correctly. Instead of giving up, I checked my spelling, file names, and code. I tested different solutions until I fixed the problem.

I selected this work because it shows the effort and progress I made during the course. It demonstrates that I became more confident and independent when solving coding problems.
